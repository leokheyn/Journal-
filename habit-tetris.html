Board

Your journal's dot grid is most likely 5 mm. Print at 100% scale (not "fit to page") so the squares line up with the dots.

Printing is turned off in this preview. To print, download habit-tetris.html from your Journal- repo and open it in Chrome, Safari or Edge.

Pieces stack from the bottom, the same way you draw them by hand. Gaps are squares the packer couldn't fill; press Shuffle layout to try another arrangement.
