Page

120 × 175 mm matches the habit board, and two fit side by side on a landscape 8.5 × 11 sheet. The dots are 5 mm apart. Print at 100% scale.

Printing is turned off in this preview. To print, download journal-pages.html from your Journal- repo and open it in Chrome, Safari or Edge.

Tap a picture space in the entry list to add your photo. Spaces left empty print as an outlined box with the title, like the one you drew for Best of the Best.
