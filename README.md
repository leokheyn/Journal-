# Journal-

## Habit Tetris

`habit-tetris.html` makes the monthly Tetris habit tracker as a printable page.

1. Download `habit-tetris.html` and open it in Chrome, Safari or Edge.
2. Paste your iPhone note into **From your notes** and press **Count habits**, or pick the month and enter the counts yourself.
   The board is a fixed size (16 × 30 by default); the space above the stack stays empty for the rest of the month.
3. Press **Print board** and print at 100% scale so the 5 mm squares match the journal's dot grid.

Each habit is one Tetris piece. The tool stacks all the pieces from the bottom with as few gaps as possible.
Use **Shuffle layout** for a different arrangement, or **Outlines only** to print it blank and color it in by hand.

The **Bingo board** prints as the left page beside the Tetris board, both on one landscape letter sheet. Tap squares as you finish them; **Still to do** lists the rest to carry over to next month.

## Journal Catch-Up

`journal-pages.html` turns the daily paragraphs in your iPhone note into printable journal pages.

1. Paste the note, enter the last day already written in **Start after** (for example `10/10`), pick the color the next weekday should get, and press **Build pages**.
2. Weekdays cycle through the four tag colors; Saturday and Sunday are combined into one entry with the weekend color.
3. Finished books, movies and "(Include some photos)" lines get picture spaces. Tap one to add your photo, or leave it empty to print an outlined box with the title.
4. Pages are 120 × 175 mm (the habit board size) and print two per landscape letter sheet. Print at 100% scale and cut along the dashed lines.
